How we work

Four steps from first call to final drawings.

You always know what you'll receive next, and nothing is final until you're happy with it.

01

Brief & measurements

A call about how you live, or how your guests should feel, plus the plans or measurements of the space.

02

Layout

A furnished floor plan with zones and circulation, so we agree on how the space works before how it looks.

03

Concept & views

Materials, colour and light, drawn as views of each key room. Two rounds of revisions are included.

04

Final set

The approved plans and views as high-resolution files, ready for your builder, your team or your listing.
